STAFF CONTROLS
Tap JINGLOVE five times within five seconds to open hidden settings.
Alt+Shift+S works with a keyboard.
Edit names and weights, then Save settings. Zero disables a prize.
At least one weight must be positive. Weights normalize to 100% internally.
Settings are stored on this browser/device only.
The menu is hidden, not password-protected; supervise the iPad.
